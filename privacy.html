Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

加油 (Add Oil) ("the app") is operated by Bagging ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. We aim to collect as little as possible.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no forms. We do not ask for or store your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or any content you create. The daily quotes are bundled in the app and chosen on your device.

Analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand basic, aggregate usage (for example, how often the app is opened and which quotes are viewed). Firebase may process anonymous usage data, app-generated identifiers (such as an app-instance ID and the vendor identifier for this device), and basic diagnostic information. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. Learn more at Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated and delivered entirely on-device; no notification data is sent to us or any server.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. We do not share data with third parties except the analytics provider described above, acting on our behalf.

Children

The app is rated for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can stop all collection by removing the app, and you can turn off notifications at any time in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@bagging.app.
